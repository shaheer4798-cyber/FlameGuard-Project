CONFIGURATION

Detection settings

01

Live fire alerts require at least 75% confidence in 2 of the last 3 camera scans. A single uploaded image needs at least 85% to raise a fire/smoke warning. In High accuracy mode an uploaded image is analyzed twice (normal and mirrored) and the results are combined, so one-off mistakes are filtered out; the live camera always uses one check per frame to stay fast. This reduces false alarms from lights and reflections, but cannot eliminate model errors.

Changes are saved automatically.

NOTIFICATIONS

Email alerts

02

When fire or smoke is detected, an email is sent to this address right away. For the live camera it includes your location and a Google Maps link. The same type of alert is emailed at most once every 2 minutes.
